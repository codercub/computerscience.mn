About Us

Codercub is the first K-12 coding academy and the International Partner of CodeAI in Mongolia.

What we do

Three ways we advance computer science.

Programs for students

From beginner courses for ages 7 and up to CS1 and the Future Intelligence Lab, built on U.S. curricula and taught in Ulaanbaatar and online.

Explore programs →

Teacher development

Training elementary and rural teachers to teach computer science, with materials in Mongolian. Since 2023, we have been CodeAI's International PD Partner in Mongolia.

U.S. partnerships

Working with CodeAI, Carnegie Mellon University and BootUp PD to bring proven curricula to Mongolia.

A word from the founder

Zolbayar Magsar

We envision Mongolia's future as a technology-driven country, so our mission is advancing computer science in Mongolia. To realize it, we work with our U.S. partners to bring the best computer science curricula to students through our academy here in Mongolia.

As a Fulbright scholar myself, with a Master's in Computer Science from the University of Missouri, I have always wanted to help bright young Mongolian minds reach their potential in the field of CS. Since founding Codercub, I have strived to create the best learning experience for our students, in terms of curriculum, teacher caliber, and learning environment. To date, we have taught more than 1,200 students and trained over 100 teachers.

— Zolbayar MagsarFounder, Codercub

Partners

Our U.S. partners

CodeAI (formerly Code.org)

A learning platform that has reached 80 million students and over 2.5 million teachers worldwide. Codercub has been its International Partner in Mongolia since 2019, and since 2023 its International PD Partner (one of 25 countries, listed here).

Carnegie Mellon University

Ranked #2 in computer science by U.S. News and #3 worldwide by QS. Through the CPCS pathway and our CS0 and CS1 courses, students can earn official CMU credit.

BootUp PD

Collaboration on teacher-training methods and materials. BootUp instructors taught at our Summer of Code 2022 (Young Coders Camp) in Ulaanbaatar.
